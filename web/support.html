Support

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Need help with The Family App? Email aleksandersandnes78@gmail.com and describe the problem, your device and the app version (shown in Settings → About). We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I invite family members? Open the Family tab and tap Share invite to send your family’s invite code.

How do I stop sharing my location? Turn off Visible on family map in Settings. You can also revoke location permission in your phone’s settings.

I forgot my password. Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen.

How do I delete my account? See Delete your account.
