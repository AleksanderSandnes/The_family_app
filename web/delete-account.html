The Family App

Delete your account

Last updated: 30 September 2026

You can permanently delete your The Family App account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  • Open Profile → Settings.
  • Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and you are signed out on that device.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email aleksandersandnes78@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, sign-in details and profile (name, email, phone number, birthday, profile photo).
  • Your wishlists and wishes, calendar events, birthdays you added, location data and device push tokens.
  • Messages you sent, your direct chats, and photos and voice messages you uploaded.

What stays with your family

Shared family shopping lists and group chats you created are handed over to another family member so the rest of your family keeps them. Other members’ own messages are not deleted. If you are the last member of your family, the family and all of its shared content are deleted too.

Backups

Deleted data can remain in encrypted database backups held by our hosting provider for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.
