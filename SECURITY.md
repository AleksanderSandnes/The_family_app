# Security policy

## Supported versions

Only the latest Android and iOS releases published from `master` receive
security fixes.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **aleksandersandnes78@gmail.com** with the subject `Family App security`.
Include the affected platform, reproduction steps and the impact observed. Do
not open a public issue and never access another family's data while testing.

You will get an acknowledgement within 5 working days and a status update at
least every 14 days until resolved. Please allow a fix to ship before any
public disclosure.

## Scope

In scope: the Android and iOS apps, the public website in `web/`, Supabase
functions, database/storage policies and this repository's CI configuration.
Out of scope: the Supabase, Vercel, Google/Firebase and Apple platforms
themselves.

Audit status: `docs/SECURITY_AUDIT.md`. Abuse or harmful content inside the app
can be reported from the app or at https://thefamilyapp.app/support.
