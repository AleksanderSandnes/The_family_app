The Family App gives your household one calm, private place for everything you coordinate together, instead of six scattered apps.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Shopping lists: add items, tick them off in the store and see what is left, live.
• Meal plans: plan breakfast, lunch and dinner for the week so nobody has to ask "what is for dinner?".
• Shared calendar: events with attendees, colours and private entries.
• Birthdays: never miss a birthday, with reminders for the whole family.
• Wishlists: each person keeps wishes with links and photos, and others can quietly reserve a gift.
• Family chat: group and direct conversations with photos, reactions and replies.
• Family map: choose to share your location with family members, and stop whenever you like.

BUILT FOR EVERY AGE
Everything is glanceable and simple enough for a ten-year-old and a grandparent. Light and dark themes, English and Norwegian Bokmål, and support for larger text and screen readers.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your family's data is only visible to your family. Location sharing is opt-in and can be switched off at any time. You can delete your account and data from inside the app or on our website.

GETTING STARTED
Create an account, start a family or join with a family code, and invite the people you live with. Free to use.
